Tetherline Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-03

Tetherline (the "app") connects an Android phone and a Windows PC over USB and relays network traffic so the PC can reach the internet through the phone's mobile data. This policy explains how the app handles information.

1. Personal information we collect

The app does not collect personal information. There is no sign-up or login, and the app sends no information to the developer's servers. It contains no advertising or analytics tools.

2. Data the app relays

  • When you tap 'Start relay', the app forwards traffic between your PC, connected over USB, and the internet through the phone's network.
  • This traffic goes only to the destinations your PC connects to. The app does not store its contents or send it to the developer or any third party.

3. Information stored on the device

The app stores only data usage counters (today / this month) on the phone so it can show them on screen. They never leave the phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

4. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet, view/change network state — to relay the PC's traffic over the mobile data network
  • Foreground service (connected device) — so relaying continues when the screen is off
  • Notifications — to show a notification while relaying is active
  • Prevent device from sleeping (wake lock) — so the device does not sleep and drop the connection while relaying

5. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not collect anyone's personal information.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
