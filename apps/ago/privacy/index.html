Ago - My routines Privacy Policy

Operator: DoAnSoft · Contact: doansoftware@gmail.com
Effective date: October 27, 2026

DoAnSoft explains below how Ago - My routines (package name: com.tannsoft.routinetimer) handles personal information.

1. Routine information and on-device storage

The routine titles, types, dates and times, display order and related settings you enter are processed in the app's storage on your device to manage routines, show times and provide home screen widgets. There is currently no feature that uploads routine content to any server operated by DoAnSoft.

If Android backup or device transfer is enabled, some app data may be backed up or transferred to another device according to the operating system's settings and policies. The purchase entitlement cache and ad frequency records are stored in an area excluded from app backups.

2. Ads and Google services

Free users are shown Google AdMob ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share device and account identifiers such as IP address, app and ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information, advertising ID and app set ID, for ad delivery, analytics, service performance and fraud prevention. The IP address may be used to estimate approximate location. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted with TLS.

This information may be processed by Google and partners involved in ad delivery according to their own policies and your privacy choices. Information may be processed outside your country of residence as part of operating Google services. See the policies below for how Google handles personal information and international data transfers.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/next-gen/privacy/play-data-disclosure
  • Google data transfer frameworks: https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks

3. Privacy choices

Depending on your region and conditions of use, a privacy choices screen is shown through Google UMP. When UMP requires a privacy options entry point, you can open it from the app's settings to change your choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. How ads are shown, or whether they are shown at all, may vary with your privacy choices and ad inventory.

Once a Remove Ads purchase is confirmed, the app stops making new ad requests and removes the ad UI.

4. Remove Ads purchase

Remove Ads purchases are processed through Google Play. The app uses the purchase state, purchase data and signature provided by Google Play to verify the Remove Ads entitlement, and stores the entitlement on the device. DoAnSoft does not use its own purchase verification server. The app never asks for or stores payment details such as card numbers. Payments and purchase history are handled according to Google Play's policies.

5. Support email

If you send a support email, your sender email address, message and any information you attach are processed to answer your inquiry and resolve the issue. Please do not include unnecessary sensitive information such as passwords, payment details or purchase tokens. Support records are deleted without delay once their purpose is fulfilled, except where the law requires them to be kept, in which case they are kept only to the extent and for the period required. Gmail is used to deliver and store email.

6. Retention, deletion and your rights

Routines stored on your device can be deleted with the app's delete feature, and local app data can be deleted by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Operating system backups and Google Play purchase history are managed separately according to each service's settings and policies. Deleting app data does not delete purchase history or mean a refund.

Retention and deletion of ad and payment information processed by Google follow that service's policies and your settings. You can send privacy questions and requests to access, correct or delete information to doansoftware@gmail.com. DoAnSoft handles requests within the scope of the information it holds and manages and the applicable law. For routines that exist only on your device, or records managed separately by Google, DoAnSoft will explain how to delete them and where to send requests.

7. Intended users

Ago is a routine management app for general users. It is not designed for or marketed to children.

8. Privacy contact and changes to this policy

Privacy contact: DoAnSoft
Email: doansoftware@gmail.com

If this policy changes, the content and effective date on this page will be updated, and any separate notice or consent required by applicable law will be carried out.
